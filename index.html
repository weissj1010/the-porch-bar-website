OUR BUILDING

Since 1901

Before it was The Porch Bar, it was a Pabst Hotel.

Our building went up in 1901 as a hotel for Pabst, one of the largest brewers in the world, and it ran until Prohibition. Over the next century it was a bakery and later Kay's Dance Studio. In 2024 we opened The Porch Bar inside it, and we're proud to keep a piece of Mukwonago's past open for business.
